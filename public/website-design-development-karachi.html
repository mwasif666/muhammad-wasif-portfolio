Website design and development in Karachi

A business website has one job: make a stranger trust you enough to get in touch. That takes a clear layout, honest copy, a page that loads before anyone gives up, and a contact route that works on a phone at 11pm. Built on solid HTML, CSS and JavaScript — the foundation everything else sits on.

  • HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript
  • Responsive on every screen
  • Bootstrap · Tailwind
  • SEO-ready markup
  • Fast by default

Design and build, not one then the other

The familiar failure: a beautiful design is signed off, then it turns out the hero text does not fit once real content arrives, the layout collapses at 380px, and the fonts add two seconds to the load. Design and development were done by different people who never spoke.

Because I do both, the design is made with the constraints already in view — how the type behaves at every width, what happens when a heading runs three lines, how much the images actually weigh. You approve something that will exist rather than something that has to be compromised on the way to being built.

What comes with every site

  • Responsive layouts tested on real phones, not just a narrow browser window.
  • Semantic HTML — proper headings, landmarks and labels, which is the same work that makes a site accessible and makes it readable to search engines.
  • SEO foundations — unique titles and descriptions per page, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt, Search Console connected on launch day.
  • Speed — right-sized images in modern formats, sensible font loading, no framework where plain JavaScript does the job.
  • Contact that works — forms that validate and actually deliver, WhatsApp and click-to-call on mobile, and tracking so you know which page brought the enquiry.

Plain HTML, Bootstrap or a framework

Not every site needs React. Petroc Energy and Pinnacle Design Agency are hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript — they load quickly because there is nothing extra to load, and they cost less to build and to host. Badaruddin uses Bootstrap for a fast, dependable responsive grid.

When a site has dozens of repeating pages, or content coming from a CMS, or interactive sections that would be painful in vanilla JavaScript, then React, Next.js or WordPress earns its complexity. Picking the lightest tool that does the job is the actual skill, and it is the one clients rarely get charged honestly for.

Written for people who will read it

Design decisions here are made against how the page will really be met: on a phone, on a middling connection, by someone who is comparing you against two other tabs. That means the first screen says what you do, the call to action is reachable with a thumb, and the site does not ask for a page load to answer a basic question.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Karachi?

It scales with pages, features and how much design work is bespoke. A focused business site is a very different budget from a store or a booking platform. Send the pages you have in mind and you get a fixed written quote — plus an honest note if a smaller build would do the job.

How long does a business website take?

A straightforward business site is typically two to four weeks from approved content. The delay is almost always content, not code — sites wait on text, photos and logos far more often than on development.

Do you design the website too, or only build it?

Both. I can design from scratch, or build from a design you already have in Figma, XD or a PDF. If you bring a design, I will flag anything that will not survive real content or a narrow screen before building it.

Will the website work on mobile phones?

Yes, and it is designed for mobile first, because that is where most of your visitors will be. Layouts are tested on real devices, not just resized in a desktop browser.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes. I can set up the domain, hosting, SSL and email, or deploy to an account you own so you keep full control — which I recommend, so you are never locked to one developer.

Will the site be set up for Google?

Every build ships with unique page titles and meta descriptions, semantic headings, structured data, a sitemap and robots.txt, and Google Search Console connected. That is the technical groundwork — ranking then depends on content and time, which no developer can promise.
